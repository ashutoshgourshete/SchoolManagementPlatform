1. Executive Summary & Market Positioning

EduManage Cloud is engineered as a high-margin, multi-tenant B2B SaaS platform for the modern educational ecosystem. It unifies primary/secondary schools (K-12) and collegiate degree institutions within a single modular code-base, eliminating fragmented software purchases.

The platform deploys a Dual-Engine Financial Architecture:

Dual Financial Architecture:
• Engine A (SaaS Provider Level): Predictable recurring subscription revenue (MRR & ARR) collected from school trusts and college groups.
• Engine B (Institution Operations Level): Granular student fee scheduling, scholarship discounting, batch invoice generation, multi-mode collections (Cash/Bank/UPI), and overdue balance aging.

SaaS Revenue Metrics & Telemetry

The built-in SaaS Provider Telemetry dashboard calculates:

  • MRR (Monthly Recurring Revenue): Total recurring fees recognized on a monthly cadence across all active tenant institutions.
  • ARR (Annual Recurring Revenue): Projected annual contract value (MRR × 12).
  • Seat Expansion Rate: Automatic upsell trigger when a tenant approaches 90% of their enrolled student limit.

3. Engine B: Institutional Fee & Revenue Billing Model (B2C)

Each subscribed institution operates an independent, compliant financial ledger to manage student admissions, tuition, custom scholarship concessions, installment plans, and digital collections:

A. Modular Line-Item Fee Structures

Institutions configure itemized fees rather than opaque lump sums: Tuition fee, Laboratory fee, Library & Activity fee, Sports fee, Examination fee, and Route-based Transportation fee.

B. Flexible Billing Frequencies

Supports Monthly (preschools/coaching), Quarterly/Term-wise (private K-12), Semester-wise (colleges), and Annual upfront payment cycles.

Scholarships & Student-Specific Concessions

Institutions can assign customized fee assignments to individual students:

  • Percentage Discounts: e.g., 10% to 50% concession for merit scholars or sports achievers.
  • Fixed Flat Concessions: e.g., Flat ₹5,000 concession with explicit audit reason recorded in the database.
  • Net Invoicing Formula: Invoice Total = (Sum of Line Items) - Discount Amount.

Multi-Channel Payment Collection Engine

The platform supports both traditional offline collections and modern digital payments:

  • Manual Cash, Cheque & Bank Transfers: Front-desk accountants record payments directly, generating sequential receipts (RCT-2026-0001).
  • Online Payment Gateway Integration: Supports instant online checkout via Razorpay and Stripe (UPI, QR Code, Debit/Credit Cards, Net Banking).
  • Webhook Deduplication & Idempotency: Online payments are only marked successful via verified cryptographic webhook events, eliminating browser redirect spoofing.
  • Partial Payments & Status Progression: Invoices dynamically transition across: Issued → PartiallyPaid → Paid → Overdue.

6. Multi-Tenant Data Isolation & Security Governance

Data security is guaranteed at the architectural level to ensure institutional privacy and regulatory compliance:

  • Row-Level Security (EF Core Global Filters): All queries execute with an automatic WHERE TenantId = @CurrentTenant predicate, preventing any cross-school data visibility.
  • Cryptographic JWT Claims: The tenant context is resolved exclusively from verified JWT token claims; user-supplied headers or URL query parameters cannot manipulate tenant access.
  • Audit Trails: Financial allocations, fee waivers, grade revisions, and login events are permanently recorded with timestamps, user IDs, and client IP addresses.
